## Data source

The data used in this analysis are publicly available indicators from the 
INKAR database (Indikatoren und Karten zur Raum- und Stadtentwicklung), 
provided by the German Federal Institute for Research on Building, Urban Affairs 
and Spatial Development (BBSR).

The dataset was cleaned and prepared for analytical purposes by the author.
Original data and documentation are available at:
https://www.inkar.de
